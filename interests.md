# Reading interests

What you want out of the arXiv panel, in your own words. Optional — delete
this file and the day's paper is simply the newest unseen match for
`[arxiv].query` in config.toml, which is what it was before.

This exists instead of learning your taste from clicks. Clicks are barely
observable here — a middle-click or "open in new tab" looks identical to
ignoring something — and a preference learned from a handful of triage
decisions is a guess in a lab coat. Writing it down works on day one, costs
nothing per day, and is wrong only in ways you can see and edit.

**How it is read.** Bullets under a heading containing *not*, *avoid*,
*exclude* or *skip* push a paper down; bullets under any other heading push
it up, and a rejection outweighs a match. Everything else here — including
this paragraph — is ignored by the ranker and kept for you.

Terms match as substrings against the title and abstract, so prefer a phrase
to a single word: `search` also matches "research".

This only reorders what `[arxiv].query` already returned. To see papers the
query never fetches, widen the query; to choose among the ones it does, edit
the lists below.
